Android TLS trust store
=======================

cacert.pem is the unmodified Mozilla CA certificate bundle distributed by curl:
https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html
https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem

Mozilla certificate data date: 2026-09-25
Downloaded bundle SHA-256:
a41b5d356aea97a529fe27e0f7316d2f9d946d75927476cf9cf1b90637d00505

License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).
The license is available at https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/ .
The upstream Mozilla source location is recorded in cacert.pem's header.

The existing Android data asset packaging includes this file automatically.
net_utils loads data/cacert.pem through SDL's APK-aware asset reader and supplies
it to libcurl using CURLOPT_CAINFO_BLOB before every transfer, including reused
sessions after curl_easy_reset. Certificate-chain and hostname verification
remain enabled. A missing or invalid bundle must fail the request; do not disable
verification to work around connection failures.

Maintenance: refresh cacert.pem from the HTTPS download URL above before releases
and when Mozilla updates its trust store. Compare the downloaded file's SHA-256
with https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem.sha256 and update the date and digest here.
Keep the bundle unmodified, including the upstream source notices.
